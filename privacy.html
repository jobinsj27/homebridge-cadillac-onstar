Privacy policy

The optional Gmail integration asks for read-only access to your mailbox. It searches for recent Google Voice forwards and reads candidate messages on your Homebridge host to match vehicle arrival and departure alerts. It cannot send or modify email.

The plugin stores its Google authorization in a private file. It persists the last known presence state, event times, and identifiers used to reject duplicates. It does not archive email bodies. Homebridge exposes the resulting presence state to your paired home. The plugin does not send mailbox contents or credentials to the project maintainer, advertisers, or analytics services.

Disable Gmail presence to stop polling. You can revoke authorization in Google Account connections. Remove the local Gmail session and presence state files and their backups to erase the stored integration data. This does not delete email from Gmail.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
